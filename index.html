Frequently asked questions

About Placeify.

Answers about the planned Placeify platform and its current development stage.

What is Placeify?

Placeify is an augmented reality shopping platform in development. It is designed to help users discover, compare and visualize products before purchasing.

Can I already download the Placeify app?

Not yet. Placeify is currently under active development and will be released when the first public version is ready.

Does Placeify sell products directly?

Placeify may display products and offers from external retailers. Users may be redirected to an external retailer to complete their purchase.

Does Placeify use affiliate links?

Placeify may use affiliate links. When a user completes an eligible purchase through such a link, Placeify may receive a commission from the retailer or affiliate network.

Which products will be supported?

Placeify initially plans to focus on categories where visualization and product comparison provide a meaningful benefit, including furniture, home products and selected electronics.

How can retailers work with Placeify?

Retailers, brands and potential partners can contact Placeify at placeify@gmx.net to discuss possible cooperation.
