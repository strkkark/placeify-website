Frequently asked questions

About Placeify.

What is Placeify?

Placeify is an augmented reality shopping platform in development. It is designed to help users discover, compare and visualize products before purchasing.

Can I already download the app?

Not yet. Placeify is currently under development and will be released when the first public version is ready.

Does Placeify sell products directly?

Placeify may display products and offers from external retailers. Users may be redirected to a third-party retailer to complete a purchase.

Does Placeify use affiliate links?

Placeify may use affiliate links. When a user purchases through an eligible link, Placeify may receive a commission from the retailer or affiliate network.
